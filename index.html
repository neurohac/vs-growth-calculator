Vestibular schwannoma growth probability

Estimates the probability of ≥20% volume growth from baseline by a surveillance scan roughly one to three years after baseline, from two early volume measurements. The follow-up scan must be at least 60 days after baseline.

Research tool. Not a medical device and not for clinical decision-making. This model was developed on 91 patients with 28 growth events at a single centre and externally validated on 32 patients with 9 events. Confidence intervals on its performance are wide, and at the operating threshold roughly half of the patients it flags do not go on to grow. It has not been prospectively validated, cleared by any regulator, or evaluated for effect on patient outcomes. Outputs are probabilities for research use only and must not replace radiological or clinical assessment.
